<p class="lede">The first event of a semester tells you everything. You walk in and you can feel whether it's going to be a real one. This one was.</p>

## the room

First event of the semester. The room was full. Not politely full, actually full. People standing along the back, friends pulling friends in, the kind of turnout you can't fake.

![the room going up at the spring kickoff](/images/kickoff/kickoff-win.jpg)

<p class="pull">A good kickoff sets the tone for everything after it. So we made the tone loud.</p>

![members connecting at the spring kickoff](/images/kickoff/kickoff-connect.jpg)

## who showed up

- New members who'd never heard of progsu until a friend dragged them in
- Returning members picking up where they left off
- Everyone left knowing at least a few more people than when they walked in

![the progsu eboard at the spring kickoff](/images/kickoff/kickoff-eboard.jpg)

None of this happens without the eboard. They built the room, ran the night, and made the whole thing look easy. This kickoff couldn't have happened without them.
