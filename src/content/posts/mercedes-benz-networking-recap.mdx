## what they covered

- Hiring timelines and what actually gets your resume read
- What it looks like to build a career at a company like Mercedes-Benz
- Paths across engineering, software, and finance
- Real answers to real questions, no corporate fluff

A 60+ turnout for a weeknight Q&A says something about this community: when a company shows up ready to be real with students, students show up ready to listen.
