## what it was

not every competition is about who ships the fastest. this one was about who thinks the clearest.

progsu teamed up with aigt to run an ideathon: a structured pitch competition where teams developed ai-driven concepts from scratch and presented them to judges.

## the challenge

- build a novel, grounded ai concept
- defend every assumption in front of people who know the space
- make it matter

## what we saw

teams came in with ideas spanning:

- healthcare
- finance
- campus life
- logistics

some were scrappy. some were genuinely surprising. all of them had to hold up under real pressure.

## takeaway

this is what early-stage thinking looks like when you take it seriously. the ideathon was the first real test of the semester, and it set the bar for everything that followed.
