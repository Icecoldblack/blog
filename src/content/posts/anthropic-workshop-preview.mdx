progsu brought anthropic to campus on april 27th for a hands-on workshop on building with claude. students didn't just watch, they built.

## what we covered

- prompt engineering: how to get reliable, useful output from an llm
- claude api integration: connecting to the api and making real requests
- building real applications: taking what you learned and shipping something by the end of the session

## who was there

open to all students regardless of experience level. the room had a mix of people who'd never touched an api before and people who were already building with ai. both left with something.

## the result

by the end of the session, students had working projects and a clearer picture of what building with ai actually looks like in practice. not a lecture. actual output.

anthropic gave every student who attended $20 in api credits to keep building after the workshop.
