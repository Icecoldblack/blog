## open teams

We're building out three teams and there's room for people who want to do real work. Not resume padding. Actual org-building.

- **Growth**: marketing, outreach, community expansion
- **Tech development**: workshops, software, educational content
- **Operations**: logistics, events, internal systems

## how to get involved

If you missed the meeting, it's not too late.

- Reach out to any exec member
- Tell them what you want to work on
- We'll figure it out from there
